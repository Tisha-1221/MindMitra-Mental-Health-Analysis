About Us

MindMitra is a wellness-focused platform designed to help individuals understand their mental and emotional well-being through data-driven insights. We combine sentiment analysis, behavioural tracking, and intelligent pattern recognition to provide a deeper view of how daily habits, moods, and lifestyle choices influence mental health.

Our mission is to empower students, working professionals, and individuals from all walks of life to proactively understand their emotional patterns and seek support when needed. We believe that mental wellness should be accessible, stigma-free, and integrated into everyday life in a simple, non-intrusive way.

Through weekly reflections, behaviour monitoring, and personalized insights, MindMitra creates a supportive space where users can gain clarity, identify stress triggers, and take small but meaningful steps toward emotional balance.

At MindMitra, we aim to be your digital companion — helping you understand your mind, track your progress, and grow toward a healthier you.
